Inside - Outside (भितर - बाहरे)

Class 1 Math-Magic
Zone A: Original (English) Textbook Source
Story: The Arab and his Camel 🐪
It was a cold winter day. The Arab 👳 was travelling on the Camel's 🐪 back. At night, the Arab pitched his tent ⛺ and went inside it. The Camel was outside.
🐪 Camel: "May I put my neck inside? It is too cold outside."
👳 Arab: "Okay! You may put your neck inside."
🐪 Camel: "May I put my front legs inside? It is too cold outside."
👳 Arab: "Okay! You may put your front legs inside."
🐪 Camel: "Can I come inside? It is too cold outside."
👳 Arab: "Oh! No the tent is too small for both of us."
🐪 Camel: "So I am coming inside and you go outside!"
⛺ 🐪 👳
Camel inside tent • Arab outside in cold
Zone B: Mundari (मुण्डारी) PALASH Verified
कहानी: अरब होड़ो आर अयाः ऊँट 🐪
रबाङ दिन रे मियाद अरब होड़ो 👳 अयाः ऊँट 🐪 चेतान रे सेन-बारा तनाय ताएकेना। निदा रे अरब होड़ो अयाः तंबू ⛺ तियाड़ केदा आर भितर बोलोयेना। ऊँट दो बाहरे रे ताएकेना।
🐪 ऊँट: "आइं अमः तंबू भितर हॉटोक बोलो दायेया? बाहरे रे पुर सुबुन रबाङा।"
👳 अरब: "ठीक गेया! अम अमः हॉटोक तंबू भितर दोहो दायेयम।"
🐪 ऊँट: "आइं माड़ंग जाङ्गा भितर दोहो दायेया? बाहरे रे पुर रबाङा।"
👳 अरब: " ठीक गेया! अम अमः माड़ंग जाङ्गा भितर दोहो दायेयम।"
🐪 ऊँट: "आइं पूरा भितर हिजुः दायेया? बाहरे रे पुर रबाङा।"
👳 अरब: "ओहो! का! तंबू दो आलं बारोन (हम दोनों) लागित पुर हुरुन गेया।"
🐪 ऊँट: "एनरेदो आइं भितरिं हिजुः तना, आर अम बाहरे सेनोः मे!"
⛺ 🐪 👳
ऊँट = भितर (Inside) • अरब = बाहरे (Outside)
Teacher Note: Use classroom door/objects to demonstrate Inside (भितर) and Outside (बाहरे). Page 2 of 5

Top-Bottom & Near-Far (चेतान-लताड़ & जपाः-संगीन)

Class 1 Math-Magic
Zone A: Original (English) Textbook Activity
1. Top - Bottom:
🏺🏺🏺 Top Pot [✓]  |  🪜🐶 Bottom Animal [✓]
2. Nearer - Farther:
🏡 🐦 Nearer Bird to house [✓]  |  🌳 🐱 Farther Cat from tree [✓]
3. On - Under (Table Scene):
🌀 Fan = Above table
🐱 Cat = On table [✓]  |  🐭 Rat = Under table [✗]
4. Rolling - Sliding:
⚽ ✏️ 🧅 Rolling Objects: Ball, Pencil, Onion
📦 🧳 🔋 Sliding Objects: Box, Suitcase, Battery
Zone B: Mundari (मुण्डारी) PALASH Verified
1. चेतान - लताड़ (Top - Bottom):
🏺🏺🏺 सबते चेतान रेयाः चटु [✓]  |  🪜🐶 लताड़ रेयाः सेता [✓]
2. जपाः - संगीन (Nearer - Farther):
🏡 🐦 ओड़ाः जपाः रेयाः चेणे [✓]  |  🌳 🐱 दारू खोन संगीन पुसी [✓]
3. चेतान - लताड़ (मेज चेतान / लताड़):
🌀 पंखा = मेज चेतान
🐱 पुसी = मेज चेतान रे [✓]  |  🐭 चेंदू = मेज लताड़ रे [✗]
4. गुदरांव - घिसड़ांव (Rolling - Sliding):
⚽ ✏️ 🧅 गुदरांव जिनिस: बॉल, पेंसिल, प्याज
📦 🧳 🔋 घिसड़ांव जिनिस: बाक्सा, सूटकेस, बैटरी
Mundari Terms: Chetan (Top), Latar (Bottom), Japah (Near), Sangin (Far), Gudrang (Roll), Ghisrang (Slide). Page 4 of 5

Wise Grandmother & Shapes (सयान बुढ़ी & आकार को)

Class 1 Math-Magic
Zone A: Original (English) Story & Geometry
🥁 Story: The Wise Grandmother
1. A Lamb was going to see his grandmother.
2. On the way, a Wolf met him: "I will eat you!"
3. Grandmother gave an idea: "Hide in a dholak!"
4. The dholak rolled down the road safely home!
📐 Geometric Shapes & Colors
▲ Triangle $\rightarrow$ Green
● Circle $\rightarrow$ Red & Pink
■ Rectangle/Square $\rightarrow$ Blue & Yellow
🎨 Shape Kit Figures
🦋 Butterfly
🐟 Fish
Zone B: Mundari (मुण्डारी) PALASH Verified
🥁 कहानी: सयान बुढ़ी आर मेंढ़ा होन
1. मेंढ़ा होन अयाः बुढ़ी नेल लागित सेनोः तनाय ताएकेना।
2. होर रे हुंडर ताकेद किया: "आइं अमके जोमेया!"
3. सयान बुढ़ी काजी किया: "ढोलक भितर उकुन मे!"
4. ढोलक गुदरांव-गुदरांव सेना आर मेंढ़ा होन सुरक्षित ओड़ाः बोलोयेना!
📐 आकार को आर रंग (Mundari Shapes)
▲ तिकोन (Triangle) $\rightarrow$ हरियर (Green)
● गोल (Circle) $\rightarrow$ आराः (Red) & गुलाबी
■ चौकोन (Rectangle) $\rightarrow$ लिल (Blue) & ससांग
